PROCESSOR LAB QUEST

Open processor-lab.html in your browser to use the complete offline simulator.
No installation, login, server, or internet connection is needed.

The original introduction video appears first. Use Pause, Replay, Sound on,
or Skip. It can also be replayed from the footer later.

Mission map: choose a topic or one of the three playable learning missions.
Full Lab: open any of the eleven original sections and select All information.
My notebook: review and export the evidence collected in your missions.

The original academic content, examples, models, settings, formulas, quizzes,
glossary, reports, and simulation source are preserved. The changes are in
presentation, navigation, and the optional learning experience around the lab.

For editing and rebuilding, see UI_REDESIGN.md.
